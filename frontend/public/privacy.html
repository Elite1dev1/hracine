Hracine (“Hracine,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) respects your privacy and is committed to protecting the personal information you provide when you visit our website, purchase our products, contact us, subscribe to our communications, or otherwise interact with our services.

This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, how we protect it, when we may share it, and the choices available to you.

By using our website, hracine.com, you agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

02

Information we collect

We may collect information that you voluntarily provide to us and information that is automatically collected when you use our website.

A. Information you provide directly

Depending on how you interact with Hracine, we may collect:

  • Full name
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Billing and delivery address
  • Shipping information
  • Order and purchase history
  • Payment and transaction information
  • Account information, where applicable
  • Information submitted through contact or consultation forms
  • Hair and scalp information voluntarily provided when using our consultation services
  • Product preferences
  • Reviews, feedback, comments, photographs, or other content you voluntarily submit
  • Information provided when subscribing to our newsletter or marketing communications
  • Any other information you voluntarily provide to us

We do not intentionally request sensitive personal information unless it is necessary for a service you have specifically chosen to use.

B. Payment information

When you make a purchase, payment information may be processed by third-party payment providers.

Hracine may receive information such as your payment status, transaction reference, payment method, and other transaction-related information, but we generally do not directly store your complete card number, PIN, CVV, or other full payment credentials.

Payment providers process your information according to their own privacy policies and terms.

When you visit our website, certain information may be collected automatically, including:

  • Browser type
  • Device type
  • Operating system
  • Pages visited

  • General location information

04

Cookies and similar technologies

Hracine may use cookies, pixels, tags, analytics tools, and similar technologies.

These technologies may help us:

  • Keep our website functioning properly
  • Remember user preferences
  • Understand website traffic and usage
  • Improve website performance
  • Measure advertising and marketing campaigns
  • Understand which products and content are most relevant to visitors
  • Provide and measure personalized advertising where applicable

Third-party providers, including analytics and advertising providers, may also use cookies or similar technologies when you visit our website.

If we use Google advertising products, Google and its partners may use cookies to serve or personalize advertising based on a user's previous visits to our website or other websites. Google requires publishers using its advertising products to disclose these practices in their privacy policies.

You may be able to manage cookies through your browser settings and, where applicable, through our cookie consent tools.
